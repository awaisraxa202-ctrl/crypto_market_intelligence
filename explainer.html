Understanding Your Market Intelligence

Everything explained in plain English. No jargon. Real examples.

01Risk Metrics — "How Much Can I Lose?"
VaR (Value at Risk)
Think of it like a "bad day" prediction. It tells you how much you could lose on a really bad day.
📌 Example: If VaR is -3.04%, it means: "On 95 out of 100 days, you won't lose more than 3.04% of your money."
✅ GOOD: Under 5% — Acceptable risk ⚠️ WARNING: 5-15% — Risk is getting high ❌ DANGER: Over 15% — Your portfolio is too risky
Risk of Ruin
This is the probability you will lose ALL your money. It's the most important risk metric.
📌 Example: If Risk of Ruin is 97.8%, it means: "If you keep trading this way, you have a 97.8% chance of blowing up your account."
✅ GOOD: Under 5% — You're safe ⚠️ WARNING: 5-15% — You're risking too much ❌ DANGER: Over 15% — STOP TRADING. Reduce position size.
Max Drawdown
The worst loss your portfolio has ever experienced from a peak to a trough.
📌 Example: If Max Drawdown is -20.77%, it means: "At the worst point, your portfolio was down 20.77% from its highest value."
✅ GOOD: Under 15% — Normal ⚠️ WARNING: 15-30% — High but manageable ❌ DANGER: Over 30% — You need to change your strategy
Sortino Ratio
This tells you how much return you're getting for the downside risk you're taking. Higher is better.
📌 Example: Sortino Ratio of 0.68 means: "You're making 0.68% of return for every 1% of downside risk."
✅ GOOD: Over 1.0 — Excellent risk-adjusted returns ⚠️ WARNING: 0.5-1.0 — Decent but can improve ❌ DANGER: Under 0.5 — Poor risk management
Calmar Ratio
This tells you how much return you're making compared to your worst loss. Higher is better.
📌 Example: Calmar Ratio of 0.36 means: "For every 1% of max loss, you're making 0.36% of return."
✅ GOOD: Over 2.0 — Excellent recovery from losses ⚠️ WARNING: 1.0-2.0 — Acceptable ❌ DANGER: Under 1.0 — Struggling to recover from losses
Kelly Fraction
This tells you the optimal percentage of your portfolio to risk on each trade to maximize long-term growth.
📌 Example: Kelly Fraction of 0.03 (3%) means: "You should risk 3% of your portfolio on each trade for maximum growth."
💡 TIP: Use HALF the Kelly number to be safe (1.5% instead of 3%).
02Signal Metrics — "What Do the Signals Mean?"
Market Mood
The overall direction of the market based on all assets combined.
📌 Example: BULLISH = Market is likely to go UP
BEARISH = Market is likely to go DOWN
MIXED = No clear direction
Fear & Greed Index
Measures market emotion. When everyone is scared (Fear), it's often a good time to buy. When everyone is greedy, it's often a good time to sell.
📌 Example: 0-25 = Extreme Fear → Contrarian BUY signal
25-45 = Fear → Good time to accumulate
45-55 = Neutral → No strong signal
55-75 = Greed → Be cautious
75-100 = Extreme Greed → Contrarian SELL signal
Ensemble Signal
A combined signal from ALL assets using weighted voting. It tells you the overall market direction.
📌 Example: "Ensemble Signal: LONG (Confidence: 78%)" means: 78% of the system's signals are pointing UP.
Conviction / Confidence
How sure the system is about a signal. Higher conviction = more confidence in the trade.
📌 Example: 72% conviction means: "We're 72% confident this trade will work."
✅ HIGH: Over 70% — Strong signal, take the trade ⚠️ MEDIUM: 40-70% — Consider taking a smaller position ❌ LOW: Under 40% — No trade, wait for better opportunity
03Trade Plan — "How Do I Execute This Trade?"
Trade Plan Components
Every trade plan has these parts:
📌 Entry: The price you buy or sell at
📌 Stop Loss: The price where you exit if the trade goes against you (protects your capital)
📌 Take Profit 1: The first target where you take 50% profit
📌 Take Profit 2: The second target where you take the remaining 50% profit
📌 Risk: How much of your portfolio you're risking on this trade
📌 R:R (Risk/Reward): How much you stand to gain vs. how much you stand to lose
Risk/Reward Ratio (R:R)
How much you stand to gain vs. how much you stand to lose on a trade.
📌 Example: R:R of 2.0 means: "For every $1 you risk, you stand to make $2."
✅ GOOD: Over 2.0 — Excellent risk/reward ⚠️ WARNING: 1.0-2.0 — Acceptable ❌ DANGER: Under 1.0 — Not worth the risk
Position Size
How much of your portfolio you allocate to a single trade. The system adjusts this based on your confidence in the trade.
📌 Example: If Position Size is 1.2%, it means: "You're risking 1.2% of your portfolio on this trade."
✅ GOOD: 1-2% per trade — Standard ⚠️ WARNING: 3-5% per trade — High risk ❌ DANGER: Over 5% per trade — You're gambling, not trading
04Performance Metrics — "Is the System Working?"
Profit Factor
This tells you if you're making more money than you're losing.
📌 Example: Profit Factor of 1.85 means: "For every $1 you lose, you make $1.85."
✅ GOOD: Over 1.5 — Profitable ⚠️ WARNING: 1.0-1.5 — Barely profitable ❌ DANGER: Under 1.0 — You're losing money
Recovery Factor
How quickly you recover from losses. Higher is better.
📌 Example: Recovery Factor of 3.2 means: "You recover 3.2x faster from losses than the market average."
✅ GOOD: Over 2.0 — Excellent recovery ⚠️ WARNING: 1.0-2.0 — Acceptable ❌ DANGER: Under 1.0 — Struggling to recover
Win Rate
The percentage of trades that win. But remember: win rate alone doesn't matter — risk/reward matters too!
📌 Example: A 40% win rate with 2:1 R:R is PROFITABLE. A 80% win rate with 0.5:1 R:R loses money.
💡 TRADER'S SECRET: A 40% win rate with 2:1 R:R = PROFITABLE (+20% net). Don't chase high win rate — chase good risk/reward.
05Advanced Data — "What's Really Happening?"
Order Book (Depth)
A real-time list of buy and sell orders waiting in the market. It shows you where price is likely to move next.
📌 Example: More buy orders (Bids) = Bullish
More sell orders (Asks) = Bearish
Balanced = No clear direction
On-Chain Metrics
Data from the Bitcoin blockchain itself — not exchanges. This tells you what whales and miners are actually doing.
📌 MVRV Z-Score: Tells you if BTC is overvalued or undervalued
📌 NVT Ratio: High = overvalued, Low = undervalued
📌 Miner Reserves: Rising = bullish, Falling = miners selling
ETF Flow Data
Shows how much institutional money is flowing into Bitcoin ETFs. Since 2024, this is the primary driver of BTC price.
📌 Example: Strong inflows = Bullish
Sustained outflows = Bearish
Trade Policy Uncertainty (TPU)
Measures how uncertain the market is about trade policies (tariffs, trade wars).
📌 Example: LOW TPU = Fundamentals drive the market (mining costs, supply/demand)
HIGH TPU = Sentiment drives the market (fear/greed, news)
06Events & Narrative — "What's Coming Up?"
Economic Calendar
A list of upcoming events that can move the markets. These are the most important dates to watch.
📌 Key Events:
🔴 FOMC Meetings — Fed decides interest rates (biggest impact)
🟡 CPI Report — Inflation data
🟡 Jobs Report — Employment data
🟢 Powell Speeches — Fed chair comments
💡 TRADER'S TIP: Reduce position size by 50% before major events. Always wait for the news to hit before trading.
Risk Scenario Planning
The system plans for different possible outcomes so you're never caught off guard.
📌 Example:
📈 SCENARIO 1: FOMC Dovish → BTC rallies 3-5%. Action: Take profit.
📉 SCENARIO 2: FOMC Hawkish → BTC drops 3-5%. Action: Buy the dip.
📊 SCENARIO 3: Neutral → BTC consolidates. Action: Hold positions.
Market Narrative (Daily Briefing)
A plain English summary of what's happening in the markets, written like a 15-year veteran trader is speaking to you.
📌 What It Covers:
• Macro environment (DXY, Gold, S&P 500)
• Technical levels (Support/Resistance)
• Sentiment (Fear & Greed)
• Upcoming events (FOMC, CPI, Jobs)
• Trader's take ("Here's what I would do...")
07Trade Explanations — "Why This Trade?"
Trade Explanation

📌 Example:

• Trend: Above SMA50 & SMA200 (Golden Cross)
• Momentum: RSI 58 + MACD bullish crossover
• Volume: 2.1x avg (strong buying)
• Sentiment: Fear & Greed 46 (Fear → Contrarian buy)
• Order Book: 65% buy orders vs 35% sell orders
• On-Chain: Exchange outflow ($120M) — accumulation

Why It Can Hit Target: Similar setups won 72% of the time.
Risk Factors: FOMC tomorrow — reduced size by 40%.
Post-Mortem Analysis (Failed Trades)
When a trade fails, the system explains WHY it failed and what to learn from it.
📌 Example:
❌ Trade Failed: AVAX Short
Why It Failed: FOMC came out unexpectedly hawkish, AVAX rallied with the broader market.
Lesson: Use wider stop-loss (5% vs 3%) during event weeks.
Improvement: Stop-loss multiplier increased from 2x to 2.5x ATR.
